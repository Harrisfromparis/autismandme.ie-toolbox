# MathFlix - Interactive Math & Literacy Learning Platform

An engaging educational platform that makes mathematics and literacy accessible and enjoyable for learners from junior school through secondary school, using interactive lessons, visual explanations, and gamified progress tracking.

**Experience Qualities**:
1. **Intuitive** - Navigation and concepts should feel natural and discoverable for young learners
2. **Engaging** - Colorful, interactive elements that maintain attention and motivation 
3. **Progressive** - Clear learning pathways that build confidence through achievable milestones

## Animations
Purposeful animations that support learning by showing mathematical relationships, guiding attention to important concepts, and providing encouraging feedback, balancing educational utility with moments of delight when students achieve milestones.

- **Purposeful Meaning**: Motion reveals step-by-step problem solving, celebrates achievements, and guides focus to learning objectives
- **Hierarchy of Movement**: 
  - Primary: Mathematical demonstrations and concept visualizations
  - Secondary: Progress indicators and achievement celebrations  
  - Tertiary: Subtle hover states and navigation transitions

## Component Selection
- **Components**: 
  - Cards for lesson modules with custom hover animations
  - Progress bars for tracking completion and streaks
  - Tabs for organizing math vs literacy content
  - Dialogs for lesson completion celebrations
  - Badges for achievement display
  - Buttons with playful but clear interaction states
- **Customizations**: 
  - Custom learning progress visualization component
  - Interactive math problem workspace area
  - Achievement badge gallery with animations
- **States**: 
  - Buttons: Soft shadows with bounce animations on interaction
  - Cards: Lift effect on hover with subtle scale transform
  - Progress indicators: Smooth fill animations with color transitions
- **Icon Selection**: Phosphor icons with rounded style for mathematical symbols, navigation, and educational concepts
- **Spacing**: Generous padding (p-6, p-8) for comfortable touch targets, consistent gap-4 to gap-6 for related elements
- **Mobile**: Card-based layout that stacks vertically, collapsible navigation, and enlarged touch targets for young learners on tablets
